Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

ClipCo is a private tool used by its owner. It uses TikTok Login Kit to connect to the owner's own TikTok account.

  • What is stored: the access tokens TikTok issues when the owner approves the connection, kept on the owner's own computer.
  • What is not stored or shared: ClipCo does not collect data from other TikTok users, sell data, or share data with third parties.
  • Revoking: the owner can remove access at any time in TikTok under Settings, then Security and permissions, then Apps and websites.
